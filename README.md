## 🏛️ About OSIS SMAFISTA

**OSIS** (Organisasi Siswa Intra Sekolah) is the official intra-school student organization of **SMA Al-Fityan School Tangerang**, better known as **SMAFISTA**. The organization serves as a platform for students to learn leadership, develop organizational skills, and actively contribute to school life.

This website is the **official digital home** of OSIS Putra SMAFISTA — a gateway where visitors can explore the journey, vision, divisions, and programs of each OSIS period.
